Who benefits: Land-heavy parcels — vacant lots, parking, and underdeveloped land — pay less under split rate. Highest-value neighbourhoods like 43623 tend to have large lots relative to structures.
Who pays more: Building-heavy parcels — dense residential and commercial — bear more burden. Suburban ZIPs like 43614 with high building-to-land ratios see the largest increases.
